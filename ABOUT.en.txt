About the designer

I'm Mart. I've played a mage for years.

I've followed Azeroth's main timeline and spent time in another branch on Huhuran, where my character is also named MART. Now I'm starting again in WoW: Forever (无限).

If this “archmage” looks like a fresh character, blame a little time magic. The memories survived; the levels, gold and gear did not. I can keep the title, but I still have to level up.

I made Azeroth Hearth to keep guild roleplay registrations, bulletins and shared layouts in one place. Members should be able to check what changed without digging through guild chat.

Time magic hasn't fixed the Lua errors yet. I'm working on those.

— Mart, Human Archmage of Azeroth

The designer plays on the China-region servers.
